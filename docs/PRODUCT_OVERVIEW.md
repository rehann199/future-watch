# Product Overview

Future Watch is the public website of a small studio that ships apps for phones, tablets and TVs: wellness and ambient apps, family games, and a personal finance app. The site presents the studio, lists the apps with their store links, and hosts the privacy policies that app stores require.

Live site: [futurewatch.co](https://futurewatch.co)

## Audience

- **People looking for an app** who want to see what exists and where to download it.
- **App-store reviewers and users** who follow a privacy-policy link from a store listing.
- **Partners and candidates** who want to know what the studio does or how to get in touch.

## Pages

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| Home | Headline, short studio statement, a strip of app names, a three-part philosophy section, a summary band and a link to all products |
| Products | The catalogue: 16 apps with category, platform tags, description, key benefits, store badges and a privacy link. Tabs filter by Android TV, Android, iOS and Apple TV, with counts |
| About | Company overview, philosophy, mission and values |
| Careers | States that no roles are open and invites speculative contact |
| Contact | Name, email, subject and message form; a thank-you panel replaces the form after sending |
| Privacy and Terms | Site-level legal pages |
| Per-app privacy policy | A dated policy for each app at a stable URL, shown beside that app's card |
| Not found | Friendly 404 |

## Core flows

1. **Find and install an app.** Open Products, choose a platform tab, read the card, tap a store badge.
2. **Check a policy.** From a store listing, open the app's privacy URL; or from the catalogue, follow the "Privacy Policy" link on the card.
3. **Get in touch.** Fill in the contact form; the browser sends it through a hosted email service and the page shows a confirmation.

## Behind the scenes

An admin area exists behind a login. It lets the team edit catalogue entries, the ad-network verification file, and view build-tracking and analytics screens. It is private, is not shown in any screenshot, and is described only at a high level in this repository.
